# PortraitVoice

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Indian rural farmers and farmer ambassadors, using a phone to turn a portrait and an authentic testimonial into a vertical video.

## Product Purpose

One portrait plus typed text, a note photograph, or a recording becomes a talking-head testimonial. Public gallery; internal entry and usage dashboards.

## Brand Personality

Syngenta; premium, approachable, concise. Preserve the supplied logo assets. No sign-in and no voice picker.

## Design Principles

A single primary action. Show three clear stages and honest progress. Keep original testimonial language. Always collect consent before generation and public gallery publication.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Native-language labels, keyboard access, large touch targets, visible errors, reduced motion, mobile-first layout.
