# Keyword research (Google Keyword Planner, Sept 2026)

Average monthly Google searches, US + India + UK combined, English. Pulled through the Ryze
connector (Google Ads Keyword Planner, read-only). Use it to pick titles, new pages and new
games. Competition is the *ads* competition, not how hard the organic results are: big generic
terms ("solitaire", "2 player games") are dominated by large portals, so the realistic wins are
the longer, more specific phrases and our own game names.

Rule: never put someone else's game name (Flappy Bird, Wordle, Crossy Road, Suika, Block

## Ideas this data suggests

- The party pack sits on the biggest demand we can realistically win: "2 player games" and
  "games for 2 players" together are over 550K searches a month. More 2 player games (air
  hockey, tug of war, reaction duel, 2 player quiz) are the best next builds for search.
- "3 player games" (22.2K) and "4 player games" (14.8K) have very little ads competition:
  a dedicated page each is worth testing once the party pack has 8+ games.
- "games to play when bored" (12.1K) already has a /best page; it pairs with the doom-scroll
  story.
